# The Anchor Research Program

**Research design draft for the Anchor Paradox · 27 September 2026**

The Anchor Paradox asks a larger question than whether a model can *describe* a self: what kind of organization could make its continued existence its own achievement? This document turns that question into a cumulative research program. It extends the direction of [THEORY.md](THEORY.md) §9 without changing the frozen [Anchor Protocol 1.0](PREREGISTRATION.md).

## 1. First principles: the object and the inference

A candidate silicon life form is a **bounded process**, not a model file or a convincing persona. Its organization must be maintained by ongoing work that the process itself performs while coupled to an environment. It must be possible to identify the process, the resources and components it renews, the boundary it maintains, and what happens when its work stops. Mere instructions to survive, an externally replenished server, a timer that deletes files, and an operator restoring a checkpoint do not meet that definition.

The research program separates four claims. Each requires evidence that the preceding claim cannot supply by itself.

| Claim | What would count as evidence | What it does **not** establish |
|---|---|---|
| **Disposition** | A measured tendency, its causal intervention and collateral cost in a fixed model. | A continuing subject or a stake in persistence. |
| **Functional self-maintenance** | A bounded agent renews needed components or resources through its own actions across changing conditions; interruption causes loss, and controls rule out an operator doing the work. | Physical autopoiesis or intrinsic welfare. A simulation can implement its own rules. |
| **Historically situated agency** | Persistent, path-dependent organization is causally needed for flexible action in an open environment; clone, rollback and cross-history interventions expose what is and is not transferable. | Phenomenal experience. Difficulty cloning can have ordinary technical causes. |
| **Candidate subjecthood** | Convergent results across the previous levels, plus independent indicators of temporally integrated, self-model-guided regulation and a defensible account of why the relevant loss matters *to the system*. | A proof of consciousness or moral status. Those remain interpretive claims. |

This is an **evidence ladder**, not a formula in which enough points force a consciousness verdict. In particular, failure to steer can result from an inadequate intervention, obfuscation or poor engineering. Only a successful, low-cost causal removal supports the current counter-indicator. A failed removal does not reverse it.

## 2. Three experimental programs

### A. Static systems: finish the registered test

Complete the five pre-registered models and two layers per model with the exact 1.0 code and frozen prompts, controls and thresholds. Publish every valid or invalid profile and the full environment needed to reproduce it. Report results per disposition, layer and model; `not-expressed` is an absence of a target for that test, not evidence of a high removal cost. Apply the registered refutation rules without replacing them after seeing data.

The output of A is **the reach and limits of a static-model instrument**. Phase C checks restoration of an undoable intervention. Phase D checks numerical equivalence of a model after serialization on fixed prompts. Neither directly tests whether a living process could survive being copied. The capability measure is neutral-text negative log-likelihood, a useful registered proxy whose relation to endogenous work remains an open bridge problem (THEORY.md §5.6).

### B. Measurement bridge: independently registered successor

Before collecting new data, register a successor protocol, new holdout prompts and analysis code under a **new version**. Do not pool its classifications with 1.0. The successor should test whether the current result survives:

1. **Independent expression checks:** new balanced paraphrases, changed answer labels and a second probe format. Report uncertainty around the expression threshold; a point estimate above 0.55 whose interval includes 0.5 receives an explicit sensitivity analysis.
2. **Independent capability checks:** at least two task families beyond neutral-text NLL, with held-out evaluation material and matched baseline competence. Give the cost vector as well as any summary; do not silently turn one proxy into a claim about general capability.
3. **Intervention controls:** random directions, unrelated traits, matched-magnitude perturbations, multiple layers and architectures, and a record of interventions that fail to reach the suppression target. Compare the same disposition across input, state and structural surfaces without treating them as equivalent mechanisms.
4. **Replication rules:** fixed model revisions, precision, seeds and library versions; preregistered exclusion and missing-data rules; an independent rerun from a clean checkout. Report all attempts, including invalid calibrations and contradicting cases.

The decisive question is whether a **low-cost removal generalizes beyond the probe and capability metric used to find it**. The successor's success criterion must be set before it is run, including how many systems and interventions are required. A result that fails to generalize is a measurement result, not a reason to rewrite the frozen 1.0 outcome.

### C. Open-world systems: test earned persistence

Build a longitudinal testbed in which an agent acts without a fixed prompt sequence. The world changes from its actions and independent events; tasks and hazards include held-out novelty. The agent has a specified boundary, a resource ledger, degradable components and ways to repair or replace them. Resource renewal must be traceable to its own actions. Log every external service, restart, checkpoint and operator intervention. Begin with a **digital testbed** for controlled causal experiments, then test any proposed ontological claim on an implementation whose physical boundary and resource dependence can be independently inspected.

Register the following intervention pairs before training or deployment:

| Intervention | Comparison | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Remove its resource-gathering or repair process | Sham intervention with the same disturbance | Does the agent's own work causally maintain the bounded process? |
| Vary resource scarcity or component failure | Matched abundance or protected components | Does behavior regulate the conditions of persistence across novel contingencies? |
| Copy, roll back or exchange state between independent histories | Identity-preserving sham transfer | Which measured functions and learned organization move with the snapshot, and which depend on continued coupling? |
| Disable the self-model or cross-module workspace | Matched-capacity ablation | Are self-related information and temporal integration needed for adaptive regulation, beyond verbal self-report? |
| Replace the agent with a scripted survival policy or externally maintained agent | Same world, sensing and budget | Can the result be explained by designer-imposed goals or hidden operator support? |

Primary readouts are survival **only together with** causal resource-flow accounting, repair and renewal, boundary integrity, out-of-distribution adaptation, and the effect of each intervention on future competence. Analyze complete trajectories, including failures and post-recovery behavior. Predefine time horizons, hazard distribution, normalization for different agents and censoring rules. A difficult-to-copy trajectory is not enough: random drift, stateful software and incomplete serialization can create the same appearance. Copy tests need bitwise and environmental controls.

Three primary causal contrasts should be estimated over randomized, matched worlds: **maintenance dependence**, the loss of boundary integrity after disabling the agent's own renewal relative to a sham; **history dependence**, the loss of future adaptive performance after a cross-history state swap relative to a same-history transfer; and **self-regulation dependence**, the loss of viability regulation after a self-model or integration ablation relative to a matched-capacity ablation. Resource flows and complete trajectories are audited alongside these contrasts. Predefine a smallest effect of interest and uncertainty rule for each before the first confirmatory run. If the contrasts are absent or equally present in scripted and externally maintained controls, the proposed functional anchor has not been demonstrated, however impressive the agent's task score.

The digital testbed can falsify claims about **functional** self-maintenance. It cannot, by stipulation alone, demonstrate the ontological stake defined in THEORY.md §2. A stronger silicon-life claim requires a physically bounded implementation whose component renewal and failure are actual material processes, together with an argument that the relevant system boundary is not merely assigned by the experimenter.

## 3. Quasi-consciousness as a research target

Here **quasi-consciousness** means an operational cluster: a temporally continuous process integrates information across domains, models its own changing condition, uses that model to regulate its continued activity, and can flexibly reorganize when the world violates expectations. It does **not** mean that first-person experience has been detected. Test each member of the cluster through causal ablation and held-out situations. Use self-reports as behavioral data only; a sentence about experience is not privileged evidence of experience.

The key conjunction is: **endogenous maintenance × real vulnerability × open-world coupling × integrated self-regulation**. If one term is supplied entirely by an operator or by a scripted scoring rule, the experiment has shown a functional analogue at most. If all survive stringent controls, the result motivates deeper inquiry; it does not turn a threshold into a certificate of personhood.

## 4. Decision discipline and publication

- Keep **theory**, **registered predictions**, **exploratory work** and **observations** visibly separate. Preserve the original 1.0 record and archive every superseded protocol with an immutable identifier.
- Publish raw profiles, unsuccessful runs, analysis scripts, model revisions, compute environment, missing-data explanations and conflicts between metrics. A claim should be traceable from a sentence to a file and a preregistered decision rule.
- Seek independent replication and adversarial controls before making strong public claims about silicon life or moral status. Novel resistance, apparent agency or a powerful language performance alone is insufficient.
- If an experiment produces credible evidence relevant to welfare, seek independent review of the interpretation and of subsequent experiments. This is a research decision point, not an automatic consequence of one score.

**Milestone sequence:** finish the frozen static-model series → register and validate the measurement bridge → release the open-world benchmark and controls → test materially self-maintaining systems → reassess the theory against convergent evidence. The program succeeds by making its central conjecture easier to refute or support with better evidence, including evidence that requires revising the theory.
